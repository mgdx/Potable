Potable shows the results of the official sanitary control of tap water in France: the sanitary conclusion of the latest sample taken on the network that serves your commune, the detail of every analysed parameter, and how they change over time.

These results are established by the regional health agencies (ARS) and published by the French Ministry of Health through Hub'Eau. Potable shows them as published: it adds no score, no rating and no opinion of its own.

<b>Features</b>

• Find your commune offline, by name or postal code: the list of communes is built into the app. “Near me” is optional: the location permission is only requested when you tap the button.
• The right distribution unit: when a commune is served by several, Potable lists them with their district names and remembers your choice.
• The latest sample: the sanitary conclusion as published by the ARS, its date, the distributor, bacteriological and physico-chemical compliance (quality limits and quality references kept apart), and key parameters explained (hardness, nitrates, pH, free chlorine), with their quality limit or reference.
• Watched substances: PFAS (sum of 20), total pesticides, TFA, vinyl chloride, lead, arsenic, perchlorate, and chlordecone overseas. For each, the latest known measure within the history period, or “not analysed since…”; and how many pesticides were looked for and quantified.
• A box on the non-compliant samples of the last 12 months.
• “Questions about this water”: your regional health agency (ARS) for a health question, the distributor for the water service.
• Every analysed parameter, grouped by family, with the published quality limit or reference, the relevance of pesticide metabolites according to ANSES, and a search in the list.
• History: the curve of a parameter over 1, 3, 5 or 10 years, with the limit drawn when there is one, and the timeline of the conclusions of past samples.
• Several followed communes, readable offline with the date of their data, to export and import as a JSON file.
• Optional alerts, off by default: each new analysis, or only unfavourable conclusions.
• In English and French; the official labels (parameters, units, conclusions) stay in French. Light, dark or system theme.

<b>What Potable does not do</b>

• No score, no rating, no ranking: Potable shows the conclusions of the ARS, in their own words.
• No health advice: the app does not tell you whether you can drink your water. If in doubt, the sheet of each commune names its regional health agency (ARS) and its water distributor.
• Your own tap is not covered: samples are taken on the distribution unit, so the state of the private pipes in your building is not included.
• The data are updated once a month: a result shown may be several weeks old.

<b>Privacy</b>

• A single server is contacted: hubeau.eaufrance.fr, over HTTPS only. Requests contain the codes of the commune and distribution unit you look up, and no identifier. The website of an ARS opens in your browser.
• Your position is never sent or stored: if you use “Near me”, it is used once, on the phone, to find the nearest commune, then discarded.
• No account and no Google service at all. No telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising. Your followed communes stay on your phone.

<b>Sources</b>

Sanitary control results: Hub'Eau “Qualité de l'eau potable” API, data from the French Ministry of Health and the regional health agencies (ARS). Communes: Découpage administratif communal (geo.api.gouv.fr) and Code officiel géographique (INSEE), under the Licence Ouverte Etalab 2.0. Metabolites: ANSES table.
